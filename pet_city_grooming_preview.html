About Pet City Grooming

Pet City Grooming is a cage-free, harm-free pet grooming salon in Brooklyn. Our caring team of master groomers provides comprehensive care for all breeds of cats and dogs — from teeth cleaning to fashionable haircuts. We maintain a personal database for each pet, use premium cosmetics, and sterilize all equipment after every client. Your pet's health and happiness is our passion.

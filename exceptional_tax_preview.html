Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for my tax appointment?
Bring government ID, W-2s, 1099s, bank statements, business expense logs, and last year's return. You can also upload them to our encrypted portal.
How fast can you file my tax return?
Standard individual returns take 48-72 hours once documents are received. IRS e-file refunds arrive in 14-21 days via direct deposit.
Do you assist with unfiled back taxes?
Yes, we specialize in back taxes, IRS wage garnishment release, installment agreements, and Offer in Compromise negotiations.

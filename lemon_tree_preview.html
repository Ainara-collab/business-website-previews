A Salon Built on Family Values

For over 50 years, Lemon Tree Hair Salons has provided families of all ages with a full range of salon services at affordable prices with convenient hours. Now the oldest and largest hair salon franchise on Long Island, Lemon Tree is launching new salons in new territories throughout the US.

Need a new hairstyle, want to freshen up with a cut, or need a conditioning treatment? We've got you covered. All our salons offer a wide variety of services, from a basic shampoo and haircut to artistic color, blowouts, and special occasion styling.

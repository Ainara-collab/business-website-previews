Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment for emergency care? +
No appointment is required for life-threatening emergencies. Walk-ins are accepted 24/7. Calling ahead at +49 30 1234 5678 allows our surgical team to prepare in advance.
How are emergency fees structured? +
Emergency consultations are billed according to the official German GOT (Gebührenordnung für Tierärzte). Most pet health insurances reimburse these fees 100%.
Do you support direct insurance billing? +
Yes, we settle directly with Agila, Allianz, Uelzener, HanseMerkur, and all major German pet insurers upon presentation of coverage confirmation.

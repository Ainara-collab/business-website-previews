About Evani Yoga

Hi! I'm Vania, founder of Evani Yoga Studio. After discovering how much yoga helped me feel more balanced, I traveled to Mysore, India to train with true devotees of the practice. There I became certified in Hatha yoga. Today, I'm happy to open our doors to all seeking strength, health, and happiness through yoga. Small class sizes, all levels welcome.

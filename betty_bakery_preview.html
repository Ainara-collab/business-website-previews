About Betty Bakery

We are a neighborhood bakeshop located in the heart of Boerum Hill, Brooklyn. With a touch of retro & modern, the 'Betty' is a tribute to all that is mid-century and deliciousness — baked goods like the treats that used to come out of our grandma's kitchen, but with an updated twist!

About Atelier Wehrle

Atelier Wehrle is an internationally active boutique landscape atelier based in Potsdam and Ascona. Founded by Manuel Wehrle, who was trained by the world's best garden designers in England, France, India, Switzerland, and Germany. In 2024, two of his gardens were voted among the best garden designs in Germany, Switzerland, and Austria. We create bespoke outdoor spaces shaped by context, history, and long-term use — where the seasons remain visible and tangible.

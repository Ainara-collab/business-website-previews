About Wishbone Studio

Wishbone Studio is a Berlin-based design and event planning agency dedicated to creating exquisite backdrops for exceptional occasions. Since 2016, we've produced unique settings, shoots, and temporary spaces across Europe — from Berlin to Lisbon, Munich, Cologne, and Zell am See. We're design-obsessed perfectionists who thrive on exceeding expectations, from the first impression to the final flourish.

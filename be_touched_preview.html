Meet Ashema

The strength of my work is in my presence while I massage. I listen to my own body as well as to the body of the client, paying attention to respiration and reacting to small movements or soft exhalations.

Since July 2007, I've had my own massage practice in Berlin-Kreuzberg. Trained in Bali, at the Esalen Institute in California, and continuously expanding my toolkit — from Somatic Integration to Craniosacral Therapy and Life Coaching.

2004: Esalen Massage training in Bali, Indonesia
2005-2007: In residence at Esalen Institute, California
2010-2013: Somatic Integration — deep connective tissue
2016: Craniosacral Therapy 3, Upledger Institut
2021: Life Coaching certification, Achology/Udemy

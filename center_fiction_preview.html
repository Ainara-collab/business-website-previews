Help & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

Can non-members visit the bookstore and cafe? +
Yes! Our ground-floor independent bookstore, cafe, and courtyard are open to the general public 7 days a week.
How do I apply for a Writers-in-Residence Fellowship? +
Fellowship applications open annually on September 1st for early-career fiction writers residing in New York City.
Are event tickets refundable or transferable? +
Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another guest or credited toward a future event with 24 hours notice.

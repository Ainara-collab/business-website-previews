Your Trusted Brooklyn Veterinarian

Animal Clinic of Bay Ridge is a small dog and cat practice, serving the pets of Bay Ridge since 1969. We're committed to providing quality veterinary care throughout the life of your pet.

We understand the special role your pet plays in your family. We treat your pet as we would our own, practicing the highest quality medicine with compassion and an emphasis on client education.

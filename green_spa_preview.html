Our Story & Heritage

Family Owned, Eco-Conscious & Deeply Rooted in Bay Ridge

Founded in 1998, The Green Spa & Wellness Center was born out of a passion for holistic health and sustainable living. Located in the heart of Bay Ridge, Brooklyn at 8804 Third Avenue, our spacious 3-floor luxury facility offers a true retreat from city stress.

As a certified Minority & Women-Owned Business Enterprise (MWBE), we take immense pride in maintaining the highest standards of client care and environmental stewardship. Every massage therapist on our team is NYS Board Certified, specializing in gentle oncology massage, pre/post-natal care, and clinical bodywork.

About Bay Ridge Music

Bay Ridge School of Music has been Brooklyn's premier music academy since 2010. With over 16 qualified instructors, we offer piano, guitar, drums, voice, violin, bass, and ukulele lessons — all in one location. Our students have even performed at Carnegie Hall! We believe music is more than lessons — it's a community where young people find themselves and grow. Month-to-month lessons, no long-term contracts. 250+ families trust us with their musical education.
